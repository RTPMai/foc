Years Past

Every Flyover Con schedule, one flight at a time. For most time blocks, two sessions run at once – one in Gate A, one in Gate B – so you could always build a day that fit what you wanted to learn.

FOC26 – April 17–18, 2026

Flyover Con's second year, and the first with a full two-track schedule.

Day 1 – April 17, 2026

8:00 AM

Breakfast // Registration // Live Decorating

Doors open. Grab coffee, get checked in, and watch the shop come alive before the first session.

9:00 AM
Gate A

Finding Your Profit Centers

Christy Shellenberger – Rock Hill Screen Printing

Revenue is fun. Profit is what keeps the lights on. A real shop case study breaking down where the money is actually made, where it's lost, and the decisions that move the needle – pricing, products, labor, and the difference between being busy and being profitable.

Gate B

From the Other Side of the Order

Megan Griffith (Moderator) – P&M Apparel, with Anna Wardenburg, Ali Hansen, Amy Benton

Real customers on what makes them choose a shop, what keeps them coming back, and what creates frustration along the way – a moderated conversation on serving, communicating with, and retaining clients.

10:30 AM
Gate A

Building Webstores That Sell

Meghan Brazzelle – Chipply

How successful shops use webstores to simplify ordering and unlock scalable sales serving teams, schools, and organizations more efficiently.

Gate B

AI for Streamlining Business

Paul Gormley – CIRAS

AI is more than a design tool. How decorators can use it to streamline everyday operations – marketing, analytics, communication, and decision-making.

11:45 AM

Lunch // Provided by PrintGrip

A break to eat, regroup, and keep the shop-floor conversations going.

1:00 PM
Gate A

Making Better Decisions With Data

Amanda Clark – P&M Apparel

Your business generates more data than you think. How to turn everyday information into actionable insights that improve operations, guide strategy, and drive results.

Gate B

Serving Your Community

Justin Sebren – Lucid Ink

Strong community relationships can be one of a shop's greatest advantages. Building local trust, supporting organizations, and turning involvement into lasting growth.

2:15 PM
Gate A

Resources for Decorators

Mark Bailey – SanMar

Tools, organizations, and industry resources decorators can lean on to improve operations, stay informed, and keep learning.

Gate B

Relentless Determination

Matt and Nate Richardson – Relentless Merchandise

The real story behind Relentless Merch's growth – the obstacles, the mistakes, the risk, and what it actually takes to build something and keep pushing when things get hard.

3:45 PM
Gate A

Why Customers Choose You (And Not the Cheaper Guy)

Ryan Toney (Moderator) – P&M Apparel, with Christy Shellenberger, Justin Sebren, Chris Clark

A panel on customer experience, professionalism, communication, and the real reasons customers choose one shop over another.

Gate B

Not All Work Is Good Work (When to Say No)

Megan Griffith (Moderator) – P&M Apparel, with Matt and Nate Richardson, Spencer Chernoff

Not every order, customer, or opportunity is worth taking. Learning when to say no is often where profitability actually comes from.

5:00 PM

Happy Hour // Presented by SanMar

Drinks, snacks, and a live band to close out day one.

Day 2 – April 18, 2026

8:00 AM

Breakfast // Registration // Live Decorating

Doors open for day two, same as day one – coffee, check-in, and the shop already running.

9:00 AM
Gate A

Mastering DTF: From First Print to Profitable Growth

Spencer Chernoff – Limitless Transfers

The full DTF journey – from understanding the technology to running it as a profitable part of the business, with practical tips on artwork, quality control, and avoiding common pitfalls.

Gate B

MultiMedia Design and Implementation

Megan Griffith – P&M Apparel

Combining multiple decoration methods – screen print, embroidery, transfers, specialty finishes – to elevate both design and perceived value.

10:30 AM

Live Decorating (Shop Wide)

The whole shop floor live and running – every station, every method, all at once.

11:45 AM

Lunch // Provided by SanMar

A break to eat, regroup, and keep the shop-floor conversations going.

1:00 PM
Gate A

Live Activations

Ashleigh & Elena Leon – The Side Garage

How shops can execute successful live printing activations that engage audiences, create memorable experiences, and build stronger brand connections.

Gate B

Selling with Empathy

Alexis Davis – P&M Apparel

Strong sales start with understanding the customer – how empathy, clear communication, and thoughtful guidance build trust and long-term relationships.

2:15 PM
Gate A

Be Bold. Be Odd. Marketing That Actually Works

Ryan Snaadt – Snaadt Media Group

Most marketing gets ignored. How to use video, content, and storytelling to get attention, build trust, and create marketing people actually pay attention to.

Gate B

Selling More Than A Shirt

Ryan Toney – P&M Apparel

Decorators often have a captive audience. How shops can expand beyond apparel with promotional products that increase order value and strengthen client relationships.

3:45 PM

Decorator Round Table

An open forum for fellow decorators on the realities of running a shop. Bring your questions, share your experiences, learn from the room.

FOC24 – April 19–20, 2024

The first Flyover Con – an open house on the P&M Apparel shop floor. One track, two days, peer-to-peer learning inside a working print shop.

9:00 AM

Breakfast // Registration // Live Decorating

Doors open. Get checked in on the shop floor while the presses run.

9:30 AM

Welcome // P&M Apparel // The Goal of Flyover Con

Jeremy Ray – Rock Hill Screen Printing · Christy Shellenberger – Rock Hill Screen Printing

Why Flyover Con exists, what makes it different, and what to expect.

10:30 AM

Custom Headwear & Cap America

Randy Argotsinger – Cap America

What decorators should know about custom headwear sourcing, decoration, and program design.

11:45 AM

Women in Screen Printing

Megan Griffith – P&M Apparel · Christy Shellenberger – Rock Hill Screen Printing · Kay Ferin

12:45 PM

Lunch

1:30 PM

DTF Printing

Hiring & Retention

Megan Griffith – P&M Apparel

Finding good people and keeping them.

3:45 PM

Lean Manufacturing

Steve Forbes – Iowa State University CIRAS

How lean principles apply to a print shop floor – eliminating waste, improving throughput, building systems that scale.

Happy Hour // Live Print // Last Call Live Podcast
