We are planning Flyover Con 2027 for April 16 and 17 at P&M Apparel in Polk City, and we are capping it at 75 people.

Before we lock the schedule, we want to hear from the shops who would actually be in the room. This takes about five minutes. Every question about what to teach genuinely shapes what we teach, and we read the written answers most closely.

You do not have to have attended before. If anything, we especially want to hear from shops who have not.
